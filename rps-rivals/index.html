How to play

✊Rock beats scissors
✌️Scissors beat paper
✋Paper beats rock
  1. Pick a hero and an arena.
  2. Choose your card — rock, paper or scissors — before the timer runs out. If you wait too long, a random card is played for you.
  3. Win the round and your rival loses a piece of health. Same card? It's a tie.
  4. Win two fights to win the match. Each fight has three health segments.

On a computer you can click the cards or use the keys 1, 2, 3 (or R, P, S).

FAQ

Is RPS Rivals free?

Yes, the game is free to play. Arenas, avatars, card skins and themes are unlocked with crystals that you earn by playing.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play straight away as a guest. With a free account your crystals, wins, hero levels and unlocks are saved, you can add friends and you can get on the weekly leaderboard.

How do I play with my friends?

Open ONLINE and choose PLAY WITH FRIENDS or HOST A ROOM, then invite your friends. You can also pick RANDOM PLAYER to play someone new.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. RPS Rivals runs in the web browser on computers, tablets and phones — no download needed. Just open joyjoy.gg.

What are crystals?

Blue crystals are earned by playing matches, daily missions and login rewards. Pink crystals are rare: you get them by inviting friends, from social missions and from special events.

How do heroes level up?

Every match you finish with a hero while logged in gives that hero XP. Wins give more XP than losses, and higher levels unlock more moves.
