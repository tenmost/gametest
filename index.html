RPS Rivals — 3D Rock Paper Scissors online on joyjoy.gg

RPS Rivals is a free 3D rock-paper-scissors battle game that runs in your web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Pick a hero, choose an arena and play your card: rock beats scissors, scissors beat paper, paper beats rock. Duel the computer or challenge your friends online, earn crystals, unlock arenas and level up your heroes.

Heroes: Neon Oni Warlord, Gearfox, Verdant Guardian, Stone Sentinel, Frostbound and Frog Ronin. Arenas include Orbital Temple, Neon City, Frozen Temple, Volcanic Cavern, Lost Jungle, Forgotten Graveyard, Crystal Nebula, Sunken Atlantis, Desert Mirage, Pirate Cove, Dragon Peak and Celestial Palace.
